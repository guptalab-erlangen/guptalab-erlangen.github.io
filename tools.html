Computational Tools & Data Science

Our lab develops open-source software and packages to answer scientific questions:

Toxfit

In Silico Cytotoxicity Assessment

An innovative kinetic simulation framework developed to capture and evaluate the temporal and dose-dependent effects of chemical exposure. By reading cell impedance dynamics, Toxfit computes precise 50% inhibitory concentrations ($IC_{50}$) while directly accounting for background cell turnover rates.

View Repository

ZEB1CRC

Single-Cell Data Infrastructure

A specialized multi-omics data package containing single-cell profiling data tailored to explore ZEB1-dependent modulation of fibroblast polarization. This resource maps out the core molecular mechanics governing inflammation boundaries and immune checkpoint blockade sensitivity in colorectal cancer.

Bee Population Simulator

Population Genetics Simulation

A computational framework designed for simulating base populations in honey bees (Apis mellifera) to support molecular genetic studies, mapping population structures, and evaluating breeding dynamics.
